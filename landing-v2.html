Why I built MOVED

I needed a tracker that worked when real life did.

I wanted something I could install, open in a gym with bad service, log quickly between sets, and close without feeling like I owed an app anything. No account wall. No streak anxiety. No five-minute setup ritual before a twenty-minute workout.

Fitness apps can be packed with features and still make the simple job harder: remember what I did and help me keep going. MOVED is my attempt to make that job boringly reliable.

“Do something. Log it. Leave.” is the whole product philosophy.
Offline had to be real.Start a session, lose signal, close the app, come back later.
Logging had to be fast.Useful during the workout itself, not homework afterward.
Progress had to survive a bad week.A missed workout does not erase the workouts that happened.
The app had to stay in its lane.Track movement. Surface context. Stop acting like your boss.
Why “anti-fitness”?

Because guilt is a terrible product strategy.

MOVED is not anti-exercise. It is anti-everything fitness apps sometimes pile on top of exercise that makes consistency feel harder than it needs to be.

ANTI

Anti-shame. Anti-streak panic. Anti-fake urgency.

A tracker should help you see your work clearly. It should not turn a missed Tuesday into a tiny personal failure notification.

01

No streak economy

Miss four days. Miss four weeks. Your previous movement still happened.

02

Everything counts

Strength, walking, cardio, mixed sessions, quick sessions. There is no morally superior workout type.

03

Useful, not needy

Track the set, glance at the trend, get a reasonable suggestion, then go live your life.

04

Your history stays yours

No account required. Workout history lives locally on your device, with backup and restore built in.

The workflow

Built for the part where you are actually working out.

1

Pick a lane

Strength, cardio, or both. MOVED helps assemble the session without turning setup into its own workout.

2

Log as you go

Enter a set, tap Add Set, and the next row is ready with your previous weight and reps.

3

Finish and move on

Your session becomes history, your totals update, and the app quietly stops demanding attention.

The actual workout screen

Made for between-set attention spans.

The active session is intentionally direct: what you are doing, what you did last time, what is logged now, and what is next. Big controls, editable sets, rest timing, and a session timer that survives closing the app.

Local-first by design

The gym Wi-Fi can be terrible. MOVED does not care.

Install it once and the core tracker is cached for offline use. Active workouts survive app closes and offline restarts. Backup and restore tools let you keep a copy of your history without creating an account.

Install MOVED

Browser app when you want it. App app when you don't.

MOVED works in your browser and can also be installed to your home screen or desktop.

iPhone / iPad

Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then launch MOVED from the new icon.

Android

Chrome → Install app

Use the install prompt when offered, or open Chrome's menu and choose Install app / Add to Home screen.

Desktop

Chrome / Edge → Install

Use the install icon in the address bar or browser menu, then launch MOVED like any other desktop app.
