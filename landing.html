Why this exists

Most fitness apps assume motivation is the problem.

MOVED assumes life happens. Training can be serious without your tracker acting like a disappointed camp counselor.

01

No streak economy

Miss four days. Miss four weeks. Your previous movement still happened.

02

Everything counts

Strength, walking, cardio, mixed sessions, quick sessions. There is no morally superior workout type.

03

Useful, not needy

Track the set, glance at the trend, get a reasonable suggestion, then go live your life.

04

Your history stays yours

No account required. Workout history lives locally on your device, with backup and restore tools built in.

The workflow

Built for the part where you are actually working out.

1

Pick a lane

Strength, cardio, or both. MOVED helps assemble the session without turning setup into its own workout.

2

Log as you go

Enter a set, tap Add Set, and the next row is ready with your previous weight and reps. Fix anything inline.

3

Finish and move on

Your session becomes history, your totals update, and the app quietly stops demanding attention.

Local-first by design

The gym Wi-Fi can be terrible. MOVED does not care.

Install it once and the core tracker is cached for offline use. Active workouts survive app closes and offline restarts. Backup and restore tools let you keep a copy of your history without creating an account.

Install MOVED

Browser app when you want it. App app when you don't.

MOVED works in your browser and can also be installed to your home screen or desktop.

iPhone / iPad

Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then launch MOVED from the new icon.

Android

Chrome → Install app

Use the install prompt when offered, or open Chrome's menu and choose Install app / Add to Home screen.

Desktop

Chrome / Edge → Install

Use the install icon in the address bar or the browser menu, then launch MOVED like any other desktop app.

A real beta, not marketing cosplay

You may find a bug. That's why you're here.

Core workout tracking, offline use, installation, and backup tools are ready for testing. Some edges are still evolving. Back up your history occasionally and tell us when something feels weird.
